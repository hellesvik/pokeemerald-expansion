# Gift Pokémon and fossils

When randomized encounters are enabled, Birch's three Route 101 starter
choices are distinct base-form Pokémon selected within the configured
generation and base-stat limits. The chosen starter receives two guaranteed
perfect IVs, with the guaranteed stats selected per save.

Steven's postgame Mossdeep gift is randomized from the first stage of a
pseudo-legendary line and is never single-stage. Castform, the Wynaut Egg, and
Birch's post-upgrade Johto starter remain unchanged.

Mirage Tower presents two distinct per-save randomized fossil choices. Fossils
are excluded from the item randomizer, and the Devon researcher revives the
selected fossil into its matching Pokémon at level 20.

When randomized encounters are disabled, the vanilla Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip,
Beldum, Root Fossil, and Claw Fossil behavior is restored.
